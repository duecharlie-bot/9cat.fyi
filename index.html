League setup

Set your league size and draft slot first — they decide the pick order, which everything else is measured against.

Snake order is derived from these. Change them before the draft starts.

Punt aggression is how readily the engine writes a category off. 0 keeps chasing all nine to the end; 10 abandons a losing category the moment it slips. 5 is a sane default.

Team names optional

Leave blank for "Team 1", "Team 2" and so on. Your own slot is highlighted.

Category weights Customize scoring

Match your league's scoring. 1 = counts normally, 0 = not scored at all. This is a league setting — punting a category you're losing is a separate, per-roster choice.

Instructions

A draft board for 9-category fantasy basketball. It ranks who's left by how much they'd help your roster, not by a generic list.

  1. Set your league up — Settings → League setup. Teams and draft slot decide the pick order everything else is measured against.
  2. Load projections — Settings → Load projections. We have 30 players out-of-the-box, see Load Projections for instructions.
  3. Log every pick, not just yours. Click a player to inspect him, click again to draft. Make sure to log Opponents' picks.

Reading the board

FitValue adjusted for your roster and for who'll be gone by your next turn. We recommend you draft from this.
#Rank by Total across the whole pool.
ADPWhere the market takes him. A player whose ADP is far below his # could be going later than he should.
TotalThe nine category Z-Score added up — the player on his own.

The panels on the right

Category Ledger — Where your team stands in each category, and the rank among drafted teams. Hover any player to see what he'd do to every line. Switch the dropdown to another team to compare head to head.

Punt Radar — Categories you're losing badly enough that chasing them costs more than conceding. Suggestions only; nothing applies until you click.

Everything saves automatically — refresh mid-draft and it picks up where you left off. Tuning sliders are under Tuning at the top.

Load projections

Last season's stats are already built in, from BoxScore Lab (CC BY 4.0). You only need this season's projections.

  1. Open a projections page and set it to show all players. Hashtag ESPN Basketball Reference On Hashtag also set Based On to Averages. On ESPN, scroll to the very bottom so every row loads.
  2. Select the whole table and copy it. Click just above the first player's name, scroll to the bottom, then shift-click after the last one. Ctrl/Cmd + C.
  3. Paste into the box below and press Replace pool. Don't tidy anything up first — headers, repeated rows and odd cells are all handled.

It reads the column headers itself, so any source works. If something doesn't parse it tells you which row and nothing is changed.
