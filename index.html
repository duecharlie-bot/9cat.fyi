Column headers are read automatically, so paste whatever the site gives you — ESPN, Hashtag, Basketball Monster, or your own CSV. Repeated header rows, doubled names, 0.573 (10.5/18.3) and 9.5/19.0 cells all parse. Don't clean anything up first.
