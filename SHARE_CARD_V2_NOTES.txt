nineCat Share Card v2 beta

What changed from v1:
- Draft grade is now based on projected head-to-head matchup win rate vs the league field.
- A 5-4 matchup win and an 8-1 matchup win both count as one win.
- Tied matchups count as half a win.
- Added S and S+ grades; a perfect 100% field win rate earns S+.
- Share card now lists the full drafted roster.
- Copy Share now includes a self-contained /s/?d=... URL.
- Added a public /s/ share page that reconstructs the result entirely from the URL.
- No backend or saved draft database is required for this beta.

Current grading thresholds:
S+ 100%
S  90%+
A+ 80%+
A  70%+
B  60%+
C  50%+
D  40%+
F  below 40%

Notes:
- The /s/ URL is intentionally longer than a database-backed short link because the result lives inside the URL.
- Social crawlers will see generic nineCat Open Graph metadata in this version; dynamic card previews would require a backend/server-rendered share endpoint later.
